# Viral Shorts Playbook (Roman Urdu)

## 1. Niche kyun "Psychology / insaan ki soch"?

- **Har insaan khud ko samajhna chahta hai** — audience limited nahi.
- **Retention high**: har line pe "yeh mere saath bhi hua" ka feel aata hai.
- **Share rate high**: log ise apne dost ko bhejte hain ("yeh to tum ho!").
- **Urdu me competition abhi kam** — English me saturated hai, Urdu me abhi space hai.
- **Infinite content**: roz naya angle milta hai (body language, habits, reaction, relationships, mind tricks).

## 2. 45-second structure (system yahi follow karta hai)

| Time | Line | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 sec | Hook | Curiosity gap — "Kya aap ne kabhi notice kiya..." |
| 3-8 sec | Promise | "Iske peeche ek simple rule hai." |
| 8-35 sec | 3-4 values | Har line = ek chhota surprising point |
| 35-42 sec | Pattern interrupt | Ek twist ya "lekin sabse important..." |
| 42-45 sec | CTA | Subscribe / comment question |

## 3. Hook templates (LLM ko yahi style di gayi hai)

1. "Kya aap jaante hain ke ... ?"
2. "Agar aap ke saath yeh hua ho, to aap akelay nahi hain."
3. "Log yeh baat aapko kabhi nahi batayenge."
4. "Yeh chhota sa sign batata hai ke koi aapko control kar raha hai."
5. "Aapki body language aapki soch se pehle bol jati hai."
6. "95% log is galti karte hain — aur phir sochte hain kya hua."
7. "Jab koi aapko ignore kare, to yeh mat karein."
8. "Ek minute, aur aapka poora nazar badal jayega."

## 4. Retention ke 3 rules

- **Har line chhoti** (1-2 sentence). Long line = boring.
- **Har 2-3 line me ek "twist"** — "lekin...", "asli baat yeh hai...", "sabse ajeeb baat..."
- **End me question** — comments aate hain: "Aap ke saath kya hua? likho niche."

## 5. Title + publish strategy

- Title 45-60 characters, curiosity + specific: "Jab koi baat kaat de — iska matlab" (better than "Psychology tips").
- Pehle **3 videos unlisted** rakhein, khud dekhein: voice, captions, timing theek hai?
- Uske baad **public**. Roz ek video, same time (system 6:30 PKT par post karta hai).
- Pehle 30 din algorithm ko data do — views kam aaye to niche/chair nahi badlein.
- **Metric dekho**: 3-second retention > 60%, average view duration > 50% = content chal raha hai.

## 6. Kabhi mat karein

- ❌ Doosre channel ki video/audio copy karna (copyright strike).
- ❌ Galat medical/legal/financial claims.
- ❌ Ek hi video ko thoda badal ke baar baar upload karna (spam policy).
- ❌ Token ya API key kisi ko bhejna / code me likhna.

## 7. Growth ka asaan formula

`Roz 1 video × 30 din × achha hook = algorithm ko signal milta hai.`

Viral koi ek video se nahi, **consistency + retention** se aata hai. System consistency already handle karta hai — aapka kaam sirf topic list ko fresh rakhna aur pehle kuch videos review karna hai.
